🫧 Chain Pop: Pop neon bubbles, then set off a full-screen chain reaction.

A wall of bouncy neon bubbles. Pop five in 1.5 seconds for OVERDRIVE, then one tap sets off a full-screen chain reaction. A tiny stress-relief toy, no sign-up.

How to play

  1. Tap or click a bubble to pop it, or drag across the screen to pop a whole row. Each pop is a crisp, bouncy sound that climbs in pitch, and the neighbouring bubbles wobble.
  2. Pop 5 bubbles within 1.5 seconds to arm OVERDRIVE. The screen border glows and the five dots at the top light up.
  3. Your next tap sends out a shockwave: every bubble on the screen pops in a chain reaction over about two seconds, with a rain of notes. Then the whole wall bounces back.
  4. Popped bubbles grow back after a few seconds, so it never runs out.

FAQ

Is Chain Pop free?

Yes. It is free, with no sign-up, no download and no ads. It runs in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers.

Does it have sound and vibration?

Yes. Every sound is synthesised live in the browser with the Web Audio API, so there are no audio files to load. On Android phones it also vibrates at the big moments; iPhone browsers do not support web vibration.

Which languages does it support?

English, 简体中文 (Simplified Chinese), Español, Français and 日本語 (Japanese). The language is picked automatically from your browser settings; any other language falls back to English.

Who made it?

Integ Life (integ.life). Chain Pop is one of five small stress-relief toys, together with Charge Burst, Void Harvest, Glass Smash, Knock on Wood. More games are at games.integ.life.

Is any personal data collected?

No account and no personal data. At most, anonymous usage statistics (page views and play counts) are collected.
